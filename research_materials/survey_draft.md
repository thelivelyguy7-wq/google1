# Phase 6.1: Prevalence Survey Draft

**Target:** 400 respondents, representative of the Google Photos user base.
**Goal:** Estimate the prevalence of imprecise-memory retrievals and first-failing nodes.

**Q1. When you use Google Photos to find an old photo, how often do you know EXACTLY when or where it was taken?**
- [ ] Almost always
- [ ] Most of the time
- [ ] Sometimes
- [ ] Rarely
- [ ] Never

**Q2. Think about the last time you couldn't find a photo right away. What was the main reason?**
- [ ] I couldn't figure out what words to type into the search bar. (Node D2)
- [ ] I searched for it, but there were too many results to look through. (Node D4)
- [ ] I scrolled and scrolled but couldn't spot it. (Node D4)
- [ ] I tried a search, it failed, and I didn't know what else to try. (Node D5)
- [ ] I think I might have deleted it, or it was on another account. (Node D6)

**Q3. When your first attempt to find a photo fails, what is your most common next step?**
- [ ] Try a completely different search word.
- [ ] Stop searching and just scroll through the main timeline.
- [ ] Check a specific album.
- [ ] Give up.
- [ ] Ask a friend or look in a different app (like WhatsApp/Messages).

**Q4. Have you ever found a photo after a long search, only to realize you had scrolled past it earlier because you didn't recognize it?**
- [ ] Yes, this happens often.
- [ ] Yes, this happened once or twice.
- [ ] No, never.
- [ ] I'm not sure.
