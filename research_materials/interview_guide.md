# Phase 5.2: 30-Minute Interview Guide & Tasks

## Section 1: The Recent Incident (10 mins)
*Anchor the participant on the specific retrieval failure they mentioned in the screener.*

1. "You mentioned in the screener that you recently tried to find a specific photo but had a hard time. Can you tell me the story of what you were looking for?"
2. "What exactly did you remember about the photo when you first opened the app?"
3. "Walk me through what you did. What did you type, tap, or scroll first?"
4. "When that didn't work, what did you do next?"
5. *(If applicable)* "Why did you decide to narrow by [Date/Keyword/Album]?"
6. "Did you ever see a photo that you thought *might* be it, but you weren't sure? How did you try to confirm it?"
7. "How did it end? Did you find it, give up, or look somewhere else?"

---

## Section 2: Task-based Tests (15 mins)
*We will use a seeded test library. The participant will interact with a dummy account on a test device.*

**Task 1 (Target: H1 - Expression Gap)**
"I want you to find a photo of a dog playing with a red frisbee. It was taken sometime in the summer two years ago. Go ahead and find it."
*Observation focus: What do they type first? Do they omit details they just heard?*

**Task 2 (Target: H2 - Recognition Gap)**
"Find the photo of the receipt from a coffee shop named 'Blue Bottle'. There are several receipts from different coffee shops."
*Observation focus: Do they scroll past it? Do they open multiple to inspect?*

**Task 3 (Target: H3 - Coarse Time Scoping)**
"Find the photo of a sunset over a mountain. You know you took it on a hiking trip, but you don't remember the year."
*Observation focus: Do they still try to use the timeline slider even without a date?*

**Task 4 (Target: H4 - Unguided Recovery)**
"Find the screenshot of a recipe for lasagna. (Note: The photo is technically not in the library, though they don't know this)."
*Observation focus: Watch their recovery strategies. Do they randomly guess keywords? Do they default to endless scrolling?*

**Task 5: Own Library Task**
"Now, on your own phone, I'd like you to find a photo from at least 3 years ago of a meal you really enjoyed. Talk out loud as you look for it."
*Observation focus: Natural habitat behavior and first failing nodes.*

---

## Section 3: Wrap-up (5 mins)
1. "When you can't find a photo, do you usually assume you deleted it, or do you assume it's there and you just can't find it?"
2. "Is there anything else about searching for photos that frustrates you?"
