# Phase 5.1: Participant Screener & Consent

## Screener Questionnaire
**Target:** 16 participants who use Google Photos and have recently struggled to find a specific photo.

**Q1. Do you use Google Photos to store your personal photos and videos?**
- [ ] Yes (Continue)
- [ ] No (Terminate)

**Q2. Roughly how many photos/videos are in your Google Photos library?**
- [ ] Less than 1,000 (Terminate)
- [ ] 1,000 - 5,000 (Quota: 4 max)
- [ ] 5,000 - 15,000 (Quota: 4 min)
- [ ] 15,000+ (Quota: 4 min)

**Q3. Think back to the last 4 weeks. Have you tried to find a specific photo or video in Google Photos that you knew existed, but you couldn't quite remember the exact date it was taken?**
- [ ] Yes (Continue)
- [ ] No (Terminate)

**Q4. What happened when you tried to find that photo? (Select the closest option)**
- [ ] I found it on the first try (Quota: 2 max - Quick Success Contrast)
- [ ] I found it, but I had to try a few different searches or scroll a lot (Quota: 6 min - Effortful Path)
- [ ] I couldn't find it, or I gave up and looked somewhere else (Quota: 4 min - Abandonment/Failure)
